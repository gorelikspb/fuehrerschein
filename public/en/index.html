Official Klasse B Exam Rules (TÜV / DEKRA)

  • 30 questions total: 20 Grundstoff (general traffic rules) + 10 Zusatzstoff (Class B specifics).
  • Fault points (Fehlerpunkte): Questions carry 2, 3, 4, or 5 points depending on severity.
  • Pass threshold: Maximum 10 fault points.
  • 5-point trap rule: Getting two 5-point questions wrong fails the exam immediately — even with 10 points total.
  • Focused drills: Practice all 91 five-point questions, 95 video questions, or 191 number & distance questions.

Official Exam Languages (Anlage 7 FeV)

You can take the official computer-based theory test in German or any of the 12 approved foreign languages: English, French, Greek, Italian, Croatian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, and Standard Arabic (Hocharabisch).

Personal interpreters are not allowed (except sign-language interpreters). Ukrainian and Kurmandschi have been added to Anlage 7 FeV with terminal rollout scheduled for April 1, 2027 (§ 76a FeV). Note that valid Ukrainian licenses can often be exchanged directly under Anlage 11 FeV without taking exams.

Essential Formulas & Numbers to Memorize

  • Reaction distance (Reaktionsweg): (Speed / 10) × 3 (at 50 km/h = 15 m).
  • Normal braking distance (Bremsweg): (Speed / 10)² (at 50 km/h = 25 m).
  • Emergency braking (Gefahrenbremsung): Normal braking distance ÷ 2 (at 50 km/h = 12.5 m).
  • Stopping distance (Anhalteweg): Reaction + Braking (at 50 km/h = 40 m normal / 27.5 m emergency).
  • Safe highway distance: "Halber Tacho" — half your speedometer reading in meters (at 100 km/h = 50 m).
  • Minimum tire tread depth: 1.6 mm across the entire tread.
  • Overtaking cyclists/pedestrians: At least 1.5 m in town (innerorts), 2.0 m out of town (außerorts).
